Menu

  • Welcome: Crispy homemade cracklings fresh from the oven, Delicate 3-year-old homemade apricot brandy served on dried apricots.
  • Soup: Traditional long-cooked chicken broth, homemade traditional pasta - čigy (gágoríky), vegetables and meat from the soup, homemade stuffing.
  • Main course: Option 1: Juicy oven-roasted pork collar (also known as pork neck or coppa). Option 2: Turkey thigh boneless with crispy skin, traditional potato puree with roasted onions, seasonal salad.
  • Buffet "Swedish" tables: Crispy Bavarian pork knee baked in the oven, Traditional stuffed cabbage, crispy oven-roasted pork belly, stuffed mushrooms, chicken perkelt, 2 types of cutlets, Potato salad, roasted potatoes, grilled vegetables.
